Native. Performant. Open Source.

Al-Muslim is crafted carefully in basic, clean Kotlin using Jetpack Compose and modern Android architecture. It features modularized components, reactive viewmodels, structured Hilt dependency injection, and WorkManager background scheduling for highly efficient updates.

Unlike massive apps filled with trackers, advertisements, and continuous background syncs, Al-Muslim remains highly compact, lighting fast, and respectfull of your device battery life.

Your Privacy is Our Core Mission

Al-Muslim operates entirely on your physical device. We do not use third-party cloud services, trackers, analytics, or external advertisements. Your location is processed solely on-device to compute high-precision prayer schedules, ensuring absolute data sovereignty.
